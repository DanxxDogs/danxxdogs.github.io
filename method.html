HOW WE SNIFF

RECEIPTS OVER VIBES.

Public scanners are fast and good at what they do. We use them as a first filter, then go further on Robinhood Chain where a generic score isn't enough. We aren't affiliated with any of the tools named here.

1. First filters: public scanners

We start with the same kind of checks anyone can run: contract checks in the style of GoPlus and Token Sniffer, buy/sell simulation in the style of Honeypot.is, and linked-wallet / holder-concentration maps in the style of Bubblemaps. A clean result there only means "nothing obvious yet". It is never a pass.

2. Going deeper on Robinhood Chain

  • Deployer history: what else this wallet launched, and what happened to those tokens.
  • Wallet families: wallets funded from the same source that buy, hold or sell together.
  • Same-block sells: coordinated exits that land in one block.
  • Serial-deployer patterns: repeated launch-and-pull playbooks, copycat names and recycled setups.
  • Tx receipts: every claim links to on-chain transactions people can check for themselves.

3. Off-chain: Pitch Patrol and Shill Wave

A lot of damage happens after the contract looks okay. Pitch Patrol covers growth-scam pitches and service sellers. Shill Wave tracks coordinated promo noise.

4. No green lights

We never call anything safe. A clean sniff, a "miss" or a resolved flag is not a free pass. NFA/DYOR.

5. Scorecard rules

  • Every flag goes on the Scorecard with the date it was flagged.
  • Every flag gets a 7-day check. Until that's written up, it stays pending.
  • Hit: the flagged risk happened on-chain. Miss: it didn't, or we got it wrong. Otherwise it's marked unclear.
  • Misses stay up. That's the point.

6. Post-mortems

Hits and misses both get a short write-up showing what we saw and what happened, starting with the Nov 5 checks.
