IN EVERY SNIFF

🪪 Declared team wallets

On launchpad v2, a creator can list wallets that skip the launch-second snipe tax. That is the team declaring its own opening-buy wallets. We read the list from the launch transaction and confirm every wallet on-chain on the token's curve contract.

  • How many wallets were declared, with short addresses
  • The share of supply they bought at launch vs what they hold now
  • A flag when the exact same list shows up on other launches

We call them "declared", not scam. Declaring is allowed and visible on-chain. We only say "dumped" when those wallets sold what they bought.

Example from Nov 11: 0x8f38…ad6b declared 31 wallets, all 31 confirmed on-chain. 30 of them bought 33.4% of supply at launch and now hold 0.0%. The exact same 31-wallet list was declared on 7 other launches.

RUG WATCH

🔁 Creator Change watch

After a launchpad v2 launch, only a few settings can still change. We scan for them every rug watch:

  • Buybacks switched on or off, flagged when a token turns them on and then off again
  • Fee-wallet moves, flagged when one wallet takes over fees on several tokens
  • Takeover proposals: timelocked fee-wallet overrides, with the date they take effect

We call these "changed", not rugged. A change can have a normal reason, so we check it before we flag anything.

From the Nov 10–11 scan (24h): 5 tokens switched buybacks on and then off, including 0x8736…2546. No takeover proposals.
