SHILL WAVE METER

SHILL WAVE CASES

A shill wave is a burst of near-identical promo posts, often from new or tiny accounts, timed to push a launch. We measure the noise around a token and put the on-chain facts next to it. Numbers only, no accusations. Loud isn't the same as fake, and quiet isn't the same as clean. We never call a token safe. NFA/DYOR.

How we measure

  • Mention volume: total X posts per hour for the project's handle or contract address over 48 hours (X counts endpoint).
  • Sample: X search returns 25 posts a page, so account-level stats come from a small sample. Case #1 sampled 75 posts. Treat those percentages as a sample, not a census.
  • Copy-paste clusters: groups of near-identical posts in the sample.
  • Account age and size: share of sampled accounts under 30 days old or under 100 followers.
  • Promo phrases: sampled posts that match our list of stock hype phrases.
  • We don't search by ticker, because X strips the $ sign and ticker words pull in unrelated posts.
  • On-chain: verification, owner, mint, pool and top-wallet share, and LP lock, read at launch from the explorer and DEX.
  • Re-checks at 1 hour, 24 hours and 7 days show whether the hype held up.
