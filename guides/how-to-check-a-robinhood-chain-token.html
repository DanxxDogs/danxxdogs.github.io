GUIDE

How to Check a Robinhood Chain Token Before You Ape

Six checks, free tools only, about ten minutes. None of them proves a token is fine. They catch the patterns we see most in our Scam DB.

NFA/DYOR

STEP 1: MATCH THE CA

Ticker and name mean nothing. Anyone can launch a token called the same thing. Only the contract address (CA) is unique.

  • Get the CA from the project's own official X account or site, not from a reply, a DM or a "trending" list.
  • Compare the whole address, not just the first and last 4 characters.
  • Search the name on DexScreener. More than one pair with the same name? Treat every one you can't match to an official source as a copycat.

Example from our records: the real TORCH CA ends …1111. A copycat "Torch" (TRCH) at 0xe323…4724 was rugged the same day (DD-033). See Real vs Fake CA.

STEP 2: CHECK THE DEPLOYER ON BLOCKSCOUT

  • Open the token on Robinhood Chain Blockscout and click the creator address.
  • Look at what else that wallet deployed. Dozens of tokens in a few hours is a bot (our DD-002 deployer launched ~120 tokens in 12h).
  • Check where it was funded from and whether earlier tokens died right after launch.
  • Cross-check the wallet against our Ledger of deployers who relaunched after LP removal.

STEP 3: WHO CONTROLS THE LP?

  • Find the pool on DexScreener, then open the add-liquidity transaction on Blockscout. Who added it?
  • If one wallet (often the deployer) added all of it and nothing shows it is locked or burned, that wallet can pull it.
  • Don't trust the liquidity number alone. We have logged tokens where DexScreener showed ~$1.19M or ~$286K "liquidity" while the pool held only a few dollars of real WETH (DD-006, DD-009).

Our DEVIN entry (DD-024) shows the pattern: the deployer added liquidity at 7:13 AM CT and removed 100% at 7:44 AM CT.

STEP 4: HOLDERS AND BUNDLES

  • Open the holders tab. Ignore the pool and the token contract; who holds the rest?
  • Look at the launch block. Many wallets buying in the same tx or the same few seconds is a bundle.
  • Fresh wallets funded from one source are usually one person.

From our DB: in "Anthropic" (ANT, DD-030, not affiliated with Anthropic) ~94% of supply was bought in the launch tx, and about an hour later 159 wallets sold within 5 seconds.

STEP 5: CAN YOU SELL?

  • Check the contract is verified on Blockscout and read it for owner, mint, blacklist or tax functions.
  • Look at recent swaps: are there real sells from different wallets, not just buys?
  • If you test, use a tiny amount you can lose. Never "approve unlimited" for a token you just found.

STEP 6: SOCIALS

  • Does the official X account post the same CA? How old is it?
  • Watch for paid boosts, raid-style replies and "connect your wallet to claim" posts. A claim page that needs your wallet is a drainer until proven otherwise.
  • Nobody legit DMs you first.

FAQ

Is there a free tool to check a Robinhood Chain token?

Yes. Robinhood Chain Blockscout, DexScreener and our /sniff bot in the Dip Club are all free. Type /sniff 0x… with the full address on the same line.

Does a clean sniff mean a token is fine to buy?

No. A sniff only reports public-data flags. No red flags is not a guarantee. NFA/DYOR.

How do I know if the LP can be pulled?

Find the add-liquidity transaction. If one wallet holds the LP position and there is no lock or burn, it can remove it.
